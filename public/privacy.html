Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Who we are

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use The Florique, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot read it.
  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address, delivery date and time slot, order notes, the items you ordered and the order total.
  • Payment status: whether a payment succeeded, with the payment reference from Razorpay. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay’s secure page and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Your cart: the items you add to your cart. For logged-in customers this is saved to your account; for guests it stays in your browser.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To take payment, prepare your order and deliver it.
  • To contact you about your order, for example if the delivery person cannot reach you.
  • To show you your order history and keep our records, including for tax and accounting.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay, our payment provider, to process your payment. Razorpay handles your payment details under its own privacy policy.
  • Our delivery partners, who receive your name, phone number and delivery address so they can deliver your order.
  • Our hosting and technology providers, who store the website’s data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to.

Cookies and browser storage

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in, and your browser’s storage to remember a guest cart. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details while your account is active. We keep order and payment records for as long as the law requires us to maintain business and tax records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account, subject to records we must keep by law. Email Garvitasoniorai@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can. This policy is intended to follow applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security

We use secure (HTTPS) connections, hashed passwords and restricted access to protect your information. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page.

Contact and grievances

For any privacy question or complaint, write to Garvitasoniorai@gmail.com.
