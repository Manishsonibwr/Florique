Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Why this policy is different for flowers

Flowers are fresh, made-to-order and perishable, so we cannot take them back once delivered. We still want you to be happy, so here is what we do when something goes wrong.

Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel for a full refund up to 24 hours before your chosen delivery date and time slot.
  • Within 24 hours of the delivery slot, we may already have bought and prepared your flowers, so we may not be able to cancel or refund the order.
  • To cancel, call +917972189283 or email Garvitasoniorai@gmail.com with your order number.

Damaged, wrong or poor-quality flowers

If your bouquet arrives damaged, is not what you ordered, or is not fresh, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the flowers and packaging. After checking, we will, at our choice, send a replacement or refund the amount you paid.

Missed or late delivery

  • If we fail to deliver within your selected date and time slot for a reason on our side, we will arrange a new delivery at no cost, or refund your order in full if you prefer.
  • If delivery fails because the address was wrong or incomplete, or the recipient could not be reached, we may not be able to refund the order. We will try to redeliver if possible.

What is not covered

  • Small differences in flower colour or size, which are natural, and replacements of similar value for unavailable flowers.
  • Flowers that were not cared for after delivery.
  • Orders that were refused by the recipient without a reason related to quality.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (card, UPI or bank) through Razorpay. They usually reach your account within 5 to 7 working days after we approve them, depending on your bank.
