Delivery policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver within Delhi NCR. Enter your full address and PIN code at checkout. If we cannot deliver to your area, we will contact you and refund your payment.

Choosing a date and time slot

At checkout you choose a delivery date and one of three time slots:

  • Morning: 9 AM to 12 PM
  • Afternoon: 12 PM to 4 PM
  • Evening: 4 PM to 8 PM

We aim to deliver within your chosen slot. Please make sure someone is available to receive the flowers.

Delivery charges

Delivery is free on orders above ₹999. Any delivery charge that applies to a smaller order is shown at checkout before you pay.

Tracking your order

Sign in and open My orders to see whether your order is confirmed, out for delivery or delivered.

If the recipient is not available

Our delivery partner will try to call the phone number you gave us. If we cannot reach the recipient, we may leave the flowers with a neighbour or security staff, or try again later in the day. Please see our refund and cancellation policy for failed deliveries.

Delays

Traffic, weather, strikes and other events outside our control can occasionally delay a delivery. We will contact you if we expect a delay.
